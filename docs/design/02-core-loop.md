# 02 — Core Loop

Status: **DRAFT — in progress** · Last updated 2026-10-12

## Three loops

### Moment to moment (seconds)
**Traverse → spot → engage.**
Run, climb and glide across terrain (Genshin). The eye catches something: a strange silhouette, a glint, a ruin, an enemy camp. Action combat with dodges, abilities and readable enemy tells. Loot pops with rarity colors (RotMG bag colors).

### Session (30–90 minutes)
**Follow a lead → explore a region → clear a dungeon → beat the boss → loot → new leads.**

1. **Pick a lead.** A relic's lore, an NPC rumor, an inscription, or just a mountain on the horizon.
2. **Explore.** Cross the region, find points of interest, fight, pick up common loot.
3. **Dungeon.** Graph-generated, themed by the region's lore, ending in a boss.
4. **Payoff.** Boss loot, maybe a relic. The relic's lore names new places and factions, which become new leads.
5. **Tinker.** Back at a forge or camp: reroll stats, compare gear, read lore, update the Reliquary.

### Long term (weeks)
- **Push outward.** Danger rises with distance from the origin and with region tier. Better loot lives farther out.
- **Complete the Reliquary.** Collect relics and resolve their leads.
- **Perfect builds.** Reroll toward ideal stats, awaken relic enchantments through item memory.
- **(Later)** Shared world: first discoveries, world-unique relics, D&D-style campaigns.

## How the pillars show up in the loop

| Loop step | Pillar |
|---|---|
| Spotting things on the horizon | Secrets all the way down |
| Relic lore → new leads | Loot that leads somewhere |
| Dungeon bosses | Bosses worth bragging about |
| Leads that are real places | Generated, never random |
| Rerolling, pushing outward | The chase never ends |
