## How to read these
- **Budget** is `ItemGenerator.Budget` in power points (pp; 1 pp ≈ one 10-damage hit every 4 s): the rarity bonus `PowerBudget.For(rarity, tier)` plus the kit allowance (weapon skill `ActiveSkill`, relic ultimate `Ultimate`). A Legendary relic gets 3 + 2 = 5 pp at tier 0 and 15 pp at tier 10; a starter sword is about 5–6 pp of plain swings. *Used* is what the effects cost.
- **Stats** roll uniformly between the normal range's ends; an **overroll** goes past the ceiling in +5% steps (P(≥ n steps) = e^−n; hidden cap 10). `+2 (+10%)` means two steps.
- **Ultimate cost** is shown twice: what the validator charges (a full resonance bar makes it cheap) and the share it takes before that relief, which the pipeline caps at 40% of the budget.
- **Cursed** relics carry a drawback (negative cost) and spend the power it buys elsewhere.
