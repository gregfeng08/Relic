## How to read these
- A boss runs its current phase's **rotation** in a loop, with a recovery window after each pattern. Phases change **on health only**; there are no enrage timers.
- Every damaging attack has a **physical tell** of at least 0.4 s (the wind-up). Rings and cones at tier ≤ 3 leave openings of at least 2× body width (1.2 m) at 6 m from the boss; beyond tier 3 the openings may tighten to 0.75 m. Lobbed (airborne) shots are flagged: they cast real shadows.
- Later phases **remix** earlier patterns (fused, lingering, returning, shattering, doubled) instead of just speeding them up.
- **Health** = 9 DPS × (1 + 0.5 × tier) × (2 + 0.3 × tier) min × 60, times (1 + 0.8 × (players − 1)) in a group. Hit damage per projectile is 10 × (1 + 0.4 × tier) (first pass; needs player HP numbers).

## 1. Isett Kellan, Archivist of Isterbarrow

*Librarian · tier 0 · 1080 HP solo (1944 for two, 3672 for four) · drops **Librarian's Essence** (INT +1)* · `boss-0a4c31c3a6e70514`

> Isett Kellan kept the archive at the Seven-Arch Kiln for the Lantern Sisterhood until the Ferry Mutiny of Cairnstead. When the wages stopped, they kept the books anyway, and nothing leaves Isterbarrow unrecorded.

*Refers to:* Isterbarrow (region), the Lantern Sisterhood (faction), the Ferry Mutiny of Cairnstead (event)

**Arena:** A round archive whose floor is one huge inlaid index, in Isterbarrow. **Hazards:** shelves that topple when struck; ink pools that leave footprints.

Fight length solo: about 2 min with top gear for the tier, 2 min with starter gear.

### Phase 1 — from full health

Rotation: Ink Spill → Line of Script → Scattered Folios (then repeats; 1.6 s recovery after each)

- **Ink Spill** · AreaDenial · wind-up 1.2 s · lasts 5.2 s
  - *Tell:* The ground under a player cracks and starts to glow; ink beads up along the floor runes.
  - *Does:* Attack: create a 2.5 m circle at a player's feet for 4 s, after a 1.2 s tell — every 0.5 s: 4 magical damage, Dazed 2 s.
- **Scattered Folios** · Ring · wind-up 1 s · lasts 4 s
  - *Tell:* Plants its feet and spreads its arms; ink beads up along the floor runes.
  - *Does:* Attack: fire a ring of 8 projectiles (6 m/s, 18 m; 0.4 m radius; 1 s wind-up) — each hit: 10 magical damage, Silenced 2 s.
- **Line of Script** · Laser · wind-up 1.4 s · lasts 2.4 s
  - *Tell:* A line scorches across the floor toward a player; ink beads up along the floor runes.
  - *Does:* Attack: create a 18 × 1.5 m line from the boss for 1 s, after a 1.4 s tell — every 0.25 s: 5 magical damage.

### Phase 2 — below 50% health

*Changes:* adds Thrown Tomes; Line of Script into Scattered Folios (fused Line of Script).

Rotation: Line of Script into Scattered Folios → Thrown Tomes (then repeats; 1.6 s recovery after each)

- **Thrown Tomes** · Lobbed · wind-up 1 s · lasts 2.6 s · **airborne** (watch the shadows)
  - *Tell:* Hurls its arms skyward; shadows bloom on the floor; ink beads up along the floor runes.
  - *Does:* Attack: loose a burst of 3 lobbed projectiles across 70° (9 m/s, 14 m; 0.6 m radius; 1 s wind-up) — each hit: 10 magical damage, (create a 1.5 m circle where it lands for 3 s, after a 0.4 s tell — every 0.5 s: 3 magical damage).
- **Line of Script into Scattered Folios** *(remix — fused Line of Script)* · Laser + Ring · wind-up 1.4 s · lasts 5.8 s
  - *Tell:* A line scorches across the floor toward a player; ink beads up along the floor runes.
  - *Does:* Attack: create a 18 × 1.5 m line from the boss for 1 s, after a 1.4 s tell — every 0.25 s: 5 magical damage; fire a ring of 8 projectiles (6 m/s, 18 m; 0.4 m radius; 2.8 s wind-up) — each hit: 10 magical damage, Silenced 2 s.

**Readability:** passed · smallest opening 3.91 m at 6 m · shortest wind-up 1 s · airborne patterns: Thrown Tomes
