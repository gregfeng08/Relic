## Regions

- **The Alwick Downs**: Wendle country. Honest work matters here, and the land remembers the Lamplit Age: peak-to-peak bridges.
- **The Kao Heights**: Teskari country. Climbing a little higher each year matters here, and the land remembers the Rekindling: fresh lines on old maps.
- **The Sibwimot Fens**: Lunnet country. Helping first and asking later matters here, and the land remembers the Lamplit Age: lamp-tower ruins.

## Factions

- **The Keepers of the Open Ledger at Hashiani Wells** *(Ochrel)*: keepers of a Reliquary. Wants to copy every returning wayfarer's journal. Sign: an open book.
- **Ruddry's Far-Walkers** *(Wendle)*: a company of explorers. Wants to reach farther than last year's company. Sign: a boot with a star on the sole. Friendly rivals: a guild of shipbuilders.
- **The Keepers of the Fiddle Shrine** *(Eskeln)*: tenders of a patron spirit's shrine. Wants to guide a wandered spirit home. Sign: a bowl of water and a leaf.
- **The Qaoum Kiln Circle of Glyphs** *(Ochrel)*: a circle of glyph scholars. Wants to draw the cleanest circle in the Wide. Sign: a quill tied with string. Friendly rivals: an order that relights the Long Roads.
- **The Guild of Fair Lines at Testaello Strand** *(Solannish)*: a guild of mapmakers. Wants to settle, once and for all, whose map of the coast is right. Sign: a pen crossing a road. Friendly rivals: a trading caravan company.

## Relics

- **Porcelain Mask of the Snowdrift** *(Teskari porcelain mask · frost · loss)*  
  Ketari Tes made this porcelain mask for someone who did not come home to Shoseo Terrace. It was kept polished anyway. Maker's mark: a kite-shaped seal in red wax.
- **The Evergreen Antler Knife** *(Aruvi antler knife · thorn · discovery)*  
  The antler knife that showed Oesa the way into the old vaults beneath Suaniashe. Maker's mark: the maker's grove-sign burned into the grain.
- **Carved Longbow of Aesa Hollow** *(Aruvi carved longbow · thorn · old-war)*  
  Carried at the Silent Field by a Concord scribe, who scratched a hedge pattern along its edge for luck. Maker's mark: the maker's grove-sign burned into the grain.
- **The Ferry Herb Satchel** *(Lunnet herb satchel · bloom · craft)*  
  A Lunnet herb satchel, made the slow way. The bud pattern worked into it was put there on purpose. Maker's mark: three dots of green paint.
- **Sesa's Carved Longbow** *(Aruvi carved longbow · thorn · old-war)*  
  Pulled from a broken Imperative construct near Teoruavel. It still tries to give orders. Maker's mark: a drop of red lacquer shaped like a seed.
- **The Firefly Reed Shield** *(Lunnet reed shield · mist · rivalry)*  
  Made to settle a bet between two Lunnet makers. Both sides say this reed shield proves them right. Maker's mark: a little frog carved somewhere hard to find.
- **The Bright Cutlass** *(Solannish cutlass · mist · celebration)*  
  Carried at the head of every festival parade in Renonmar, and dropped in the river at least once a year. Maker's mark: a knot of blue thread wound around the grip.
- **Clay Flask of the Lodestar** *(Ochrel clay flask · star · wonder)*  
  Carry it anywhere near Kairad and it hums like a kettle about to boil. Nobody knows why, but the kettles there are excellent. Maker's mark: a small star etched into the glass.
- **Doryael's Kite-Blade** *(Teskari kite-blade · stone · discovery)*  
  Dug out of a fallen lamp-tower near Yenrian Spire, still warm. Nobody at the Ledger recognizes the hand that made it. Maker's mark: a kite-shaped seal in red wax.
- **Reed Shield of Lilo Lilypads** *(Lunnet reed shield · mist · wonder)*  
  On clear nights it glows like a lamp-tower in miniature. The children of Lilo Lilypads call it a pocket star. Maker's mark: a little frog carved somewhere hard to find.

## Events

- **The Founding of Kilun**: Aon of Elanoani's Copying House relit a cold hearthstone and founded Kilun around it.
- **The Finding of the Generous Desert Veil**: Baduesh found the Generous Desert Veil in the ruins at Darunesh, and the Reliquary is still arguing about it.
- **The Lilpond Race**: The Lamp-Tower Fair Committee of Nimmire and the Pibap Ferry Grove-Tenders have raced to Lilpond every spring for 20 years, and the loser buys the cider.
- **The Mimsy Lilypads Expedition**: The Harbor Keel Guild set out for Mimsy Lilypads 65 years ago, and their letters still arrive, each from farther away.
- **The Lunnet Crossing**: A band of Lunnet families crossed into the Fenkin Fens and settled at Boreed, bringing their recipes with them.

## A festival

**The Frog Choir at Mogwade** (Lunnet, spring)

The whole marsh sings back to the frogs. The frogs usually win. The custom here goes back to Lulwin, who first served watercress soup to the whole of Mogwade and refused to stop. This year the Kin of Wimkinmire Farm are in charge, and the prize is a reed shield marked with three dots of green paint.
